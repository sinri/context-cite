Bioland is a fictional country created for the purpose of the **Bioland** video game series.
In this series, Bioland is a country that exists in the fictional world of the game.
It is a country with a rich history, culture, and natural resources.
In this country, people live in a harmonious coexistence with nature.
Findois is the capital and largest city of Bioland, where the king of Bioland resides.
Firewis is the second-largest city in Bioland, known for its beautiful beaches and scenic views.
The player is a young man named Vilma who is visiting the country, his travel starts from Firewis to Findois.
